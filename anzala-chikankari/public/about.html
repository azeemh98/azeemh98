Our Story

Preserving the 400-year-old craft of Lucknowi chikankari

Anzala Chikankari was born from a deep love for the hand embroidery traditions of Lucknow. Every kurti, saree and suit in our collection is embroidered by hand by artisan families who have practiced this craft for generations — passing needle and thread skills from mother to daughter, master to apprentice.

We work directly with karigars (artisans) in the old city of Lucknow, ensuring fair wages and preserving authentic stitches like bakhiya (shadow work), phanda, jaali and murri. No two pieces are ever quite identical — that's the beauty of handwork.

Whether you're shopping for an everyday kurti or a bridal ensemble, our team is happy to guide you — book an appointment for a personal styling session at our Chowk boutique, or shop the full collection online with doorstep delivery across India.
